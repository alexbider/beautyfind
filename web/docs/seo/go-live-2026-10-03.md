## Pause and the new writing rules (2026-10-04)

At 06:50 UTC the drain-mode worker job (Actions run 37182432887, in batch 3 of 17) was cancelled and the self-check trigger disabled, on the product owner's request, so the queue could be rebuilt under new rules. One in-flight listing may have lost its call; it is re-queued with the rest. The remaining "ניקוי תיאורים" runs stay in the database until the re-queue step cancels them (`--cancel-queued`).

Prompt version `2026-10-04.1` (`src/lib/import/editorial.ts`):

- **Record language banned.** The validator (`RECORD_PATTERNS` in `src/lib/import/textRules.ts`, code `record`) and the prompt reject רשומה, ברשומה, ברשומת, מצוין, מצוינת, מציינת, שמצוין, נרשם, נרשמה, מופיע כ, מופיעה ב, תוארו, מתואר, איננה מפרטת, אינה מפרטת, אין פירוט and לא מפורט, with Hebrew word boundaries and the common prefixes. The text speaks about the business directly. The same patterns apply to meta descriptions and to the cleanup script, so stored texts with record language are now counted as failing.
- **Tiered length.** The packet's richness score sets the tier (`lengthTier`): sparse 150 to 200 words, normal 220 to 320, rich 350 to 500. The tier is named in the user message, the checks flag `short` and `long` against the tier, and the repair message quotes the tier's bounds. The writer cannot pick a longer tier.
- **Proofreading.** After a draft passes, one short call (`PROOFREAD_PROMPT`) fixes spelling and grammar only and reports unknown or invented Hebrew words. The corrected text is merged back only when the FAQ count, paragraph count, numbers, Latin names and word counts (within 5 percent) are unchanged and every check still passes; a draft with unknown words is rejected and the listing keeps its text.
- **Addresses.** `src/lib/import/address.ts` translates the common Israeli street names from a fixed dictionary before the packet is built (Derech Raziel 5, Netanya becomes דרך רזיאל 5, נתניה); a street the dictionary does not know is dropped, so the packet carries the Hebrew city alone. Any draft with St, Rd, Ave, Blvd, Street, Road, Avenue, Derech, Rehov, Sderot or Kikar fails (`LATIN_ADDRESS`, code `address`).
- **Re-queue.** `description-cleanup` takes `--rewritten-since <date>` (re-queue listings whose import record carries a writer draft from that date on under an older prompt version), `--limit N` (one review batch spread across the tiers) and `--cancel-queued` (cancel the earlier queued runs). The workflow passes these through its new `extra` input. The per-listing run budget default is 0.12 USD to cover the proofreading call.

**First batch under the new rules (run 12b6b32f, 10 listings, 07:11 to 07:35 UTC, 0.98 USD).** Six drafts came from Claude and were proofread; four calls were cut off at the 16000-token cap (`max_tokens`) and fell back to the unpublished template. Sonnet 5's tokenizer spends about a token per Hebrew syllable, so a rich draft with FAQs and forty service summaries plus the model's reasoning no longer fits. Of the six, one translated a Latin business name into Hebrew, one padded a packet that had only hours and a rating, and the rich tier was reached by a listing whose only strength was a long service list. Fixes, prompt version `2026-10-04.2`: the request streams with a 32000-token cap and `effort: low`; the business name must appear exactly as the packet spells it and a draft without it is rejected; the rich tier starts at a richness score of 50 (people, prices or the business's own words on top of services) and normal at 18; `insufficientEvidence` applies when the packet has no services and none of the business's own words, so such a draft is stored unpublished rather than padded; a clean draft outside its tier gets one more length repair and, if still outside, is stored but not published (`publishBlockers`); every form of מופיע is record language; a title regex no longer reads "סדר השעות" as a doctor's name; "ניסיון רב" and its variants join the banned phrases.

## Prompt 2026-10-04.5: grounding, medical gate, floors (2026-10-04, from 10:00 UTC)

- Tiers: sparse 40 to 150, normal 70 to 280, rich 180 to 500 words.
- Fact grounding (`GROUNDING_PROMPT`, `scripts/import/editorialCall.ts`): after a draft passes the text rules, one call lists every factual claim in the description and FAQs (treatment, purpose, credential, staff, equipment, location, audience, hours, price, rating, year, language, access, contact, kind of business) and marks each supported or unsupported by the packet. Unsupported claims get one repair that removes them and a second grounding pass; a draft still carrying one is rejected (`unsupported_claims`). The claims are logged on the import record (`unsupportedClaims`, `grounded`) and in the worker log.
- Medical gate: the packet carries `medicalResponsible` (the listing has a medical responsible, or an active doctor or nurse on the business's staff, or a team member whose role names a doctor, nurse or surgeon). Without it the medical category names leave the packet and the services' categories, the prompt forbids presenting the business as medical or placing a doctor, nurse or "קוסמטיקאית מוסמכת" there, and `medicalClaims` (code `medical:`) blocks publication of a draft that does. Staff report: workflow task `medical-report` (`npm run import:medical-report`) lists live listings with published medical treatments and no medical responsible or medical staff.
- Near-duplicate treatments count once (`treatmentFamily`: "Acne treatments" and "Acne facial" are one), both in the packet and in the richness score.
- `seoName` strips a leading profession word (קוסמטיקאית, מאפרת, ספרית and the like) before the brand; a title such as ד״ר stays.
- The worker no longer fills metaTitle or metaDescription from drafts; during a rewrite it clears a stored metaTitle over 60 characters, with a pipe or the old "| מחירים וביקורות" tail, and a stored metaDescription that fails the meta rules, so the page builder's text takes over.
- `description-cleanup --rerun <run id>` queues exactly the listings of an earlier run again.

Prompt version `2026-10-04.1` (`src/lib/import/editorial.ts`):

- **Record language banned.** The validator (`RECORD_PATTERNS` in `src/lib/import/textRules.ts`, code `record`) and the prompt reject רשומה, ברשומה, ברשומת, מצוין, מצוינת, מציינת, שמצוין, נרשם, נרשמה, מופיע כ, מופיעה ב, תוארו, מתואר, איננה מפרטת, אינה מפרטת, אין פירוט and לא מפורט, with Hebrew word boundaries and the common prefixes. The text speaks about the business directly. The same patterns apply to meta descriptions and to the cleanup script, so stored texts with record language are now counted as failing.
- **Tiered length.** The packet's richness score sets the tier (`lengthTier`): sparse 150 to 200 words, normal 220 to 320, rich 350 to 500. The tier is named in the user message, the checks flag `short` and `long` against the tier, and the repair message quotes the tier's bounds. The writer cannot pick a longer tier.
- **Proofreading.** After a draft passes, one short call (`PROOFREAD_PROMPT`) fixes spelling and grammar only and reports unknown or invented Hebrew words. The corrected text is merged back only when the FAQ count, paragraph count, numbers, Latin names and word counts (within 5 percent) are unchanged and every check still passes; a draft with unknown words is rejected and the listing keeps its text.
- **Addresses.** `src/lib/import/address.ts` translates the common Israeli street names from a fixed dictionary before the packet is built (Derech Raziel 5, Netanya becomes דרך רזיאל 5, נתניה); a street the dictionary does not know is dropped, so the packet carries the Hebrew city alone. Any draft with St, Rd, Ave, Blvd, Street, Road, Avenue, Derech, Rehov, Sderot or Kikar fails (`LATIN_ADDRESS`, code `address`).
- **Re-queue.** `description-cleanup` takes `--rewritten-since <date>` (re-queue listings whose import record carries a writer draft from that date on under an older prompt version), `--limit N` (one review batch spread across the tiers) and `--cancel-queued` (cancel the earlier queued runs). The workflow passes these through its new `extra` input. The per-listing run budget default is 0.12 USD to cover the proofreading call.

## Sitewide Hebrew wording and the database outage (2026-10-04 23:00 to 2026-10-05 07:15 UTC)

**The wording fix (commit 1ed5420).** Prices in the category aggregates are a trimmed mean: prices beyond 1.5 IQR of the quartiles are dropped, the rest averaged, three prices minimum (`src/lib/stats.ts`, the same rule in SQL in `averagePrices`); the method notes on the treatment, region, city and category pages and the about pages describe it, and "חציון" is gone from the templates. Every "average" written with אמצעי became ממוצע ("המחיר הממוצע", "דירוג ממוצע בגוגל"), while "אמצעי יצירת קשר" stays. Counts read in natural Hebrew per category (`src/lib/seo/businessNoun.ts`: "3 מספרות", "מספרה אחת", "שתי מרפאות אסתטיקה", "6 עסקי יופי ואסתטיקה"). "דירוג Google" and "ב־Google" became "בגוגל" (Google Maps keeps its name), prices are written "180 ₪" everywhere (`nis` in `src/lib/format.ts` and the other formatters), and the city is "תל אביב-יפו" with a hyphen in the catalog, the UI, the meta tags, the schema and the breadcrumbs. The writer prompt (`2026-10-04.8`) bans במידה ו, הינו/הינה/הינם, אשר, כמו כן, אמצעי in the sense of an average, דירוג Google, ב־Google and חציון; the validator flags them as `wording` in every mode (so a draft with one of them is not published), the meta rules accept both "דירוג Google" and "דירוג בגוגל" in the ratings line, and `seo:verify` reports the pages with banned wording and a per-phrase table.

**The outage.** The Neon endpoint `ep-small-bird-b23u1ifd` (eu-central-1) stopped answering at about 23:15 UTC on 2026-10-04 and came back at about 06:05 UTC on 2026-10-05, almost seven hours. The owner approved the stored-content apply during the outage; a scheduled check every 30 minutes carried the sequence until the database answered. Nothing in the code caused it and nothing was lost: the rewrite runs stayed queued in the database and the worker resumed them.

About the clinic.

Glow Medspa is a physician-led medical aesthetics clinic in Yorkville, operating since 2014. Treatments are delivered by registered nurses under the direct supervision of the medical director, Dr. Amara Osei, MD CCFP, who is on site six days a week.

The clinic runs three treatment rooms and a dedicated laser suite with Candela GentleMax Pro and PicoWay platforms, and holds a diamond-level Allergan account, so injectors train annually on current Botox and Juvéderm technique.

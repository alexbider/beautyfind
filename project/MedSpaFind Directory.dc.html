{{ guideTitle }}

Toronto's medical aesthetics market spans hospital-affiliated dermatology practices in Yorkville, boutique injectable studios along Queen West, and multi-location laser chains in North York and Etobicoke. Price and technique vary widely between them, so the useful comparison is rarely the headline rate.

In Ontario, injectables are prescription treatments. A physician or nurse practitioner must assess you and authorise the product, even when a registered nurse performs the injection. Ask who the medical director is and whether they are on site during treatment hours.

What to ask before booking

  • Which device or product brand is used, and how old is the unit?
  • Is the consultation free, and is it with the person who will treat you?
  • What is the total number of sessions quoted, not just the per-session price?
  • What is the policy on touch-ups within two weeks?

About Glow Medspa

Glow Medspa is a physician-led medical aesthetics clinic in Yorkville, operating since 2014. Treatments are delivered by registered nurses under the direct supervision of our medical director, Dr. Amara Osei, MD, CCFP, who is on site six days a week.

The clinic runs three treatment rooms and a dedicated laser suite with Candela GentleMax Pro and PicoWay platforms. We are a diamond-level Allergan account, which means our injectors train annually on current Botox and Juvéderm technique.

  • Free 20-minute consultation with your treating injector
  • Written treatment plans with total session counts, not per-visit pricing
  • Complimentary two-week touch-up review on all neuromodulator treatments
